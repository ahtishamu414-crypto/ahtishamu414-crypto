### About

I'm a BSCS student at the **University of Management and Technology, Lahore**, currently in my
5th semester, and the **GeeksforGeeks Campus Mantri** for my campus. My focus is machine learning,
data science, and agentic AI — I'd rather ship a working model or agent than leave it in a
notebook. I've worked as an ML/data intern across three companies:

- **AgileTech Studio** — AI Intern
- **DevelopersHub Corporation** — Data Science & Analytics
- **DecodeLabs** — Data Analytics

I have build an agent that controls real ESP32 hardware over natural language, and
picking up JavaScript, Django, and backend fundamentals on the side. Open to internships.

### Selected Work

<details open>
<summary><b>Agentic IoT Controller</b> — an LLM wired to real hardware</summary>
<br/>

An agent (Claude tool-calling) that reads live ESP32 sensor data — a DHT22 temperature/humidity
sensor — through a FastAPI bridge, reasons over it, and flips a real relay in response to natural
language. Ships with a Streamlit chat UI and a logging dashboard.

`Python` `FastAPI` `Claude API` `ESP32` `Streamlit`
&nbsp;&nbsp;[Repo →](https://github.com/ahtishamu414-crypto/agentic-iot-controller)
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>AI Fashion Stylist</b> — final year project, team build</summary>
<br/>

A virtual personal shopper combining computer-vision wardrobe scanning with occasion, weather, and
color-aware outfit recommendations, plus LLM-generated styling explanations, a capsule wardrobe
generator, and virtual try-on.

`React/Next.js` `FastAPI` `PyTorch` `CLIP/YOLO` `PostgreSQL`
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>AI Meeting Intelligence Assistant</b> — solo build</summary>
<br/>

Ingests meeting recordings, transcribes with speaker diarization via Whisper, extracts decisions
and action items with an LLM, and pushes them into Jira. Working toward a searchable meeting
knowledge base with semantic search.

`Python` `FastAPI` `Whisper` `Jira API` `LLM extraction`
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>ML Portfolio Projects</b> — self-directed builds</summary>
<br/>

Credit risk prediction (FastAPI + Streamlit), customer segmentation (K-Means, PCA, t-SNE), time
series forecasting (ARIMA, Prophet, XGBoost), and insurance-charge regression.

`scikit-learn` `XGBoost` `Streamlit` `Pandas`
</details>
